About the UHV Cell

The Universal Human Values Cell at NBKRIST aims to create awareness about human values, harmony, responsible living, and holistic development among students and faculty.

Universal Human Values encourage individuals to understand themselves, their relationships with others, society, and nature. The objective is to support a meaningful and harmonious way of living.

Self Exploration

Understanding our thoughts, desires, expectations, and the purpose of life.

Human Relationships

Developing trust, respect, affection, care, and mutual understanding.

Harmony in Nature

Understanding our responsibility towards nature and sustainable living.
